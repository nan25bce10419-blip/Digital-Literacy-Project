Stay Safe Online – Prevention Checklist

General Safety Tips

Use strong and unique passwords for all accounts
Do not click on suspicious links or unknown messages
Never share personal information such as OTP, bank details, or passwords
Be aware of social engineering tactics used by scammers
Keep your mobile apps and system updated regularly
Avoid making financial transactions on public Wi-Fi networks

UPI / Financial Safety Tips

Never share your UPI PIN with anyone
Remember: You do not need to enter a PIN to receive money
Do not scan QR codes from unknown or untrusted sources
Banks and payment apps never ask for OTP or PIN over calls
Reporting Cybercrime

If you become a victim of cybercrime, take immediate action:

Report the incident on the National Cyber Crime Portal (https://cybercrime.gov.in)
Call the Cyber Crime Helpline: 1930
Inform your bank immediately to block further transactions

Final Note

Being alert and informed is the best way to stay safe online. Always verify before you trust any digital transaction request.
