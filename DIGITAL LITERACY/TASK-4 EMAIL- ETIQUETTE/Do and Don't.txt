##### **Responsible Social Media Use – Checklist**



As a college student, it is important to use social media responsibly. Below are some key Do's and Don'ts to follow:







* ###### &#x20;**Do's**



\* Use respectful and polite language while posting or commenting.

\* Verify information before sharing it to avoid spreading misinformation.

\* Protect your privacy by adjusting account settings and limiting personal details.

\* Use social media for learning, networking, and skill development.

\* Think before posting — consider the long-term impact on your reputation.







* ###### &#x20;**Don'ts**



\* Do not share fake news or unverified information.

\* Avoid posting offensive, abusive, or inappropriate content.

\* Do not overshare personal information like phone number or address.

\* Avoid engaging in online arguments or cyberbullying.

\* Do not waste excessive time on social media; maintain a healthy balance.
